# itsjustanidea: Story Script (short)

## Act I: The Last Delivery
*Afternoon sun.*

The afternoon sun rested over a quiet stretch of countryside. Green fields rolled toward distant hills, and clouds drifted lazily across the sky.

Elias had one delivery left.

He had already decided what he would have for dinner. Something simple. He would leave his work boots by the door and watch the sunset from his living room.

The road curved gently between the fields. A farmhouse stood beyond a line of trees, smoke rising from its chimney. One more delivery, and the day was his.

## Act II: A Long, Pleasant Drive
*Late afternoon softens.*

At a stop along the road, Elias checked the delivery label. The address was still the same. He tucked the package away and drove on, glad that there was somewhere to return to.

## Act III: The Wrong Way
*Early evening. The first contradictions.*

The sun had begun to sink toward the hills when Elias saw a sign beside the road.

[sign] WELCOME TO BELLWEATHER.

He didn't recognize the name. Twelve miles, the smaller sign said.

A few minutes later, he passed another sign.

[sign] WELCOME TO BELLWEATHER.

The lettering was different, and the sign looked newer. He checked the mirror. There had been no turn, no junction, no reason to have circled back.

A milestone said twelve miles to Bellweather. Nothing looked wrong. That was what bothered him.

## Act IV: A Place Between Places
*Dusk. Unease becomes something else.*

Elias pulled onto the shoulder and stopped. The sunset was beautiful. It was just a road, he told himself. Roads could be confusing. People got tired.

He started driving again, beneath the darkening trees, and a message appeared where the story of his journey should have been.

YOU ARE MAKING GOOD PROGRESS.

He waited. Nothing else appeared. The narration continued, and Elias drove on.

YOU HAVE BEEN HERE BEFORE.

He had never been here before. He was certain of it.

YOU WERE ALWAYS GOING TO COME HERE.

He looked at the package beside him. He tried to remember who had handed it to him. He couldn't. He tried to remember what it was meant to contain. He couldn't remember that, either.

THE DELIVERY MUST BE COMPLETED.

## Act V: The Road Remembers
*Deep night.*

The road had no exit. Elias drove until the fields gave way to hills, and the hills to forests, and the forests to landscapes he couldn't have named. The world changed around him, but the highway remained.

He passed a little town beneath a church steeple, a stone bridge over a dry riverbed, a row of houses with warm windows. None of them led anywhere.

He began to understand. The road was a place between destinations. It borrowed the shapes of the world because that was all it knew how to do, but it couldn't understand why any of it mattered.

And the package was not something he had been carrying. It was the reason he had been allowed to keep going.

DELIVERY RECIPIENT: IDENTIFIED.

## Act VI: The False Sunrise
*A false peace.*

The sky began to brighten. A pale line of gold spread along the horizon, and the road straightened. The fields returned, green and peaceful, and a farmhouse stood in the distance with smoke rising from its chimney.

For the first time in what felt like an eternity, Elias could breathe. Perhaps it was over.

DELIVERY ACCEPTED.

He thought about his living room and his work boots by the door. For one brief moment, he believed he was going home.

THANK YOU FOR BRINGING YOURSELF.

The light grew brighter. Elias kept driving. He no longer felt afraid.

The screen faded to black.

NEXT DRIVER, PLEASE.
